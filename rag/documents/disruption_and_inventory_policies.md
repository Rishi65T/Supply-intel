# STRATEGIC SUPPLY CHAIN DISRUPTION PROCEDURES & MITIGATION PLAYBOOK

## 1. Single-Source Component Disruption Protocol
- Identification Threshold: Any supplier classified as Tier-1 single-source (e.g., SUP-IND-183) with capacity utilization > 90% and lead-time variability > 3.0 days.
- Escalation: Trigger Severity Level 1 Warning to Procurement Council.
- Tactical Mitigation:
  1. Authorize emergency 40-50% purchase volume diversion to pre-qualified alternate suppliers (e.g., SUP-IND-104 Bengaluru Hub).
  2. Reserve buffer slots on CONCOR electric container rakes along Western DFC.
  3. Pre-emptively adjust factory assembly sequences at Pune Chakan MegaFactory (FAC-IND-201) to prioritize vehicle variants with available bill of materials.

## 2. Port Congestion & Customs Hold Procedures
- Threshold: Seaport congestion > 20% or dwell time > 72 hours at JNPT or Mundra.
- Action: Reroute inbound oceanic consignments to Pipavav or Hazira deepwater terminals.

# INVENTORY REORDERING & SAFETY STOCK POLICIES

## 1. Safety Stock Calculation Standard
- Formula: SS = Z * sqrt( L * sigma_D^2 + D^2 * sigma_L^2 )
- Target Service Level: 95% (Z = 1.645) for critical powertrain components; 90% (Z = 1.28) for standard commodities.
- Reorder Point Formula: ROP = (Average Daily Demand * Lead Time) + Safety Stock.

## 2. Festive Pre-Build Policy (Diwali Surge)
- Mandatory buffer inventory buildup starts 6 weeks prior to Navratri / Diwali peak season.
- Minimum Days of Inventory (DOI) required at Bhiwandi Mega-Hub: 35 days.
