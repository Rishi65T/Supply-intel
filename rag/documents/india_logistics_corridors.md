# INDIA LOGISTICS CORRIDORS & STRATEGIC INFRASTRUCTURE SOP

## 1. Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (DFC)
- Route: Dadri / Rewari to Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority (JNPT / Nhava Sheva).
- Key Industrial Nodes: Sanand, Mehsana, Palanpur, Vadodara, Surat, Mumbai.
- Traction: 25 kV AC Electric traction allowing high-speed double-stack container trains (75-100 km/h).
- Resilience Protocol: When NH48 highway faces monsoon landslides (specifically Khandala Ghats or Valsad-Navsari flooding), auto OEM components must transition to Roll-On Roll-Off (RO-RO) or containerized wagons at Palanpur or Vadodara rail freight terminals.

## 2. NH48 Mumbai-Pune-Bengaluru Golden Quadrilateral
- Highway characteristics: Heavy commercial vehicular density, steep ghat gradients in Khandala and Lonavala.
- Weather Risk: High vulnerability during Southwest Monsoon (June to September).
- Mandatory Rule: If GPS telemetry indicates stationary transit > 6 hours on Ghat sections, trigger immediate alternate supplier activation at Bengaluru Electronic City hub (SUP-IND-104).
